🔒 Privacy & Security

NordVPN's no-log policy has now been independently audited four separate times. The most recent audit, completed by Deloitte in early 2025, confirmed that no user activity logs are retained. Four audits is the strongest verification record of any provider we review — most competitors have one or two, and several have none at all.

Jurisdiction is a genuine strength here. NordVPN is incorporated in Panama, which sits outside the 5 Eyes, 9 Eyes and 14 Eyes intelligence-sharing alliances and has no mandatory data retention law. This is a materially better position than providers based in the EU, UK, US or Australia, where a court order could in principle compel forward-looking logging.

On protocols and features: NordLynx is NordVPN's WireGuard implementation, built with a double NAT system so WireGuard can run without storing user IP addresses on the server. Threat Protection blocks malware, trackers and ads at the network level. Double VPN routes traffic through two servers for a second encryption layer, and Onion over VPN sends traffic into the Tor network after the VPN tunnel. Meshnet allows encrypted peer-to-peer links between your own devices.

🎬 Streaming & Unblocking

NordVPN unblocked every streaming platform in our test set — all 8 services we check, with no failures. Netflix worked on US, UK, Japanese and Australian catalogues, which is a harder test than it sounds: regional Netflix libraries are among the most aggressively VPN-filtered services online.

BBC iPlayer is the single toughest unblocking test we run, because it detects and blocks VPN traffic more aggressively than any other major platform. NordVPN worked reliably across all 15 UK server locations we tested — not a single location failed. For UK expatriates and travellers, that consistency is the strongest argument for choosing NordVPN over a cheaper provider.

One caveat that applies to every VPN, not just this one: streaming results reflect the state of play at the time of testing. Platforms update their detection continuously, and a server that works today may be blocked next month. NordVPN has historically been quick to rotate affected IP ranges, but no provider can guarantee permanent access to any given service.
